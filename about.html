The house

A trading floor with a factory heartbeat.

CEO / Founder: Kshitij Singh Rajput · Established in 1881

We were founded to do a simple thing well: move honest cargo, and make what we promise.

KHSHAGNOR Import & Export Private Limited began in 1881 from Patna — a city on a river that has always understood trade — and grew into a house that buys, sells and manufactures across continents.

The name is our own. It is meant to sound like a house, not a slogan: a place you can send a specification to, and get a shipment back. Today the registered office remains in Bihar; the corporate desk sits in Mumbai; operations live at the ports.

We are not a marketplace and not a freight-only forwarder. We take principal positions, run production programmes, and stand behind the lot.

Principles

What we will not trade away.

Three rules. They decide which cargo we take, and which we decline.

01

The document is the cargo.

If the paperwork cannot survive a bank, a customs officer and a surveyor, the goods have not really shipped. We write as carefully as we pack.

02

Specification before speed.

A fast wrong lot is more expensive than a slow right one. Moisture, gauge, GSM, purity — measured, not assumed.

03

One desk, one name.

The person who takes the brief owns the vessel date. We do not dissolve into a chain of unaccountable subcontractors.

Production

We make, so we understand making.

Contract manufacturing is not a side door at KHSHAGNOR. Partner mills and our own packing lines let us control calendar, packing and QC — and speak to factory managers as peers, not tourists.

From private-label food packing to textile programmes and light engineering, production is how we keep promises that pure trading cannot.

Timeline

Fourteen years, written in lanes.

A house grows the way a port does — berth by berth.

2012

The house opens in Patna

First books in agri and general merchandise. Registered office established in Bihar.

2015

Mumbai corporate desk

Banking, letters of credit and a permanent presence near India’s principal container ports.

2017

Production programmes begin

First private-label packing lines and textile contract manufacturing for export.

2019

Gulf and East Africa lanes

Jebel Ali and Mombasa become weekly, not occasional. Metals and rice join the book.

2022

ISO systems & Singapore desk

Quality and environment systems certified. A liaison desk opens for ASEAN procurement.

2026

A fuller industrial book

Machinery, chemicals and project cargo sit beside the original agri and textile trades.

People

Operators, not passengers.

KHSHAGNOR is run by trade leads, production managers, documentation officers and survey coordinators. We hire people who have stood on a jetty at 2 a.m. and still filed the COO at dawn.

There is no celebrity founder page here. The house is the brand. If you work with us, you will know your desk by name — and they will know your cargo by lot number.
