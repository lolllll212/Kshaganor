Method

How a Kshaganor
shipment is built.

Every programme — whether a single container or a year-long production run — follows the same sequence.

Brief

Specification, Incoterms, volume and destination. We write the ticket before we move a rupee.

Source / make

Vendor shortlist or factory booking. Samples, mill certificates, and a locked production calendar.

Assure

In-line and pre-shipment inspection. Holds on moisture, gauge, GSM, purity — whatever the lot demands.

Clear

IEC, GST, customs, certificates of origin, phytosanitary and letter-of-credit discipline.

Deliver

Ocean, air or multimodal. Tracking to the consignee, with aftercare if anything is off-spec.
