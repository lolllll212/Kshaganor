Agri & food

Origin lots of rice, wheat, maize, pulses, spices and processed foods. Moisture, broken grain, admixture and residual chemistry are written into the contract — and checked before stuffing. Private-label packing for Gulf and African retail programmes.

Yarn, greige, dyed and finished fabrics, home textiles and apparel programmes. We work mill-side on GSM, count, colour fastness and shrinkage, then pack for the destination’s labelling rules.
