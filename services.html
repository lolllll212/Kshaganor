Import

Inbound, without surprises.

We build a vendor shortlist, lock Incoterms, and run pre-shipment inspection at origin. Cargo is insured, classified, and landed with a file your finance team can audit.

  • HS classification and duty planning
  • Vendor KYC and mill visits
  • FOB / CIF / DDP structures
  • Bonded and domestic onward movement

Export

Indian origin, world manners.

From farm or mill to FCL. We prepare the commercial set, negotiate LCs, and book space on lanes we already run — not ones we discovered this morning.

  • Buyer due diligence and payment terms
  • DGFT, drawback and RoDTEP where due
  • COO, phytosanitary, health certificates
  • Survey and sealing at load port

Production

Capacity you can calendar.

Partner plants and packing lines under Kshaganor production management. You receive a Gantt, not a hope. Samples are sealed; bulk matches the sample.

  • Contract manufacturing & OEM
  • Private label packing
  • In-line QC and AQL sampling
  • Export packing, pallets, fumigation

Aftercare

The lot is ours until you accept it.

Claims, shortages and off-spec are handled by the same desk that booked the vessel. We do not vanish behind a forwarding agent’s voicemail.

  • Arrival inspection coordination
  • Insurance claims support
  • Replacement or credit against evidence
  • Seasonal forecasting for repeat programmes

Engagement

Start with a ticket, not a meeting.

Send commodity, quantity, specification, origin or destination, and preferred Incoterm. We return a structured offer: price basis, lead time, inspection plan and document set.

Production briefs should include artwork or technical drawings, target monthly volume, and packing standard. We will tell you honestly if we cannot hold the spec.
